@"
fix(webodm): add LZW compression and tiling to gdalwarp reprojection

run_gdalwarp was called with no creation options, causing gdalwarp to
write uncompressed stripped TIFFs by default. This decompressed the
already-compressed WebODM orthophoto asset and rewrote it without
compression, producing outputs ~2.4x larger than necessary (~3.96 GB
vs ~1.67 GB for the same orthomosaic).

Adds -co COMPRESS=LZW, TILED=YES, BLOCKXSIZE=512, BLOCKYSIZE=512,
BIGTIFF=IF_SAFER, and NUM_THREADS=ALL_CPUS to the gdalwarp command.
Output size should now match the WebODM UI export (~1.6-1.8 GB).
"@ | Out-File -Encoding utf8 commit_msg.txt
